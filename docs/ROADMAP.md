# MVP roadmap

The roadmap protects one causal product story. Dates may change; gate meaning
and trust boundaries do not.

## Gates

| Gate | Outcome | Exit condition | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| R0 — Reboot | shared product/control frame and a clean execution model | Owner can explain the repository and approve the first slice | Done |
| R1 — Continuity | durable project identity, evidence and restart view | real restart experience accepted by Owner | Done |
| R2 — Cognition | evidence-backed cross-time reflection, correction and invalidation | Owner judges it more valuable than a summary | Done |
| R3 — Bounded Agency | revision-bound proposal, approval, deterministic effect, receipt and rollback | real reversible effect accepted by Owner | Done |
| R4 — Controlled Presence | one bounded public Guest encounter connected to the Owner's local Forme | complete encounter passes Technical Review and Owner Experience Acceptance | Active |
| R5 — Hardening | clean install/run, privacy sweep, failure rehearsal and repeatable story | release candidate repeats without a new feature cut | Waiting on R4 |

## R4 product outcome

R4 is one Demo-critical Hero Encounter:

1. the Owner publishes an allowlist-only Projection;
2. a curator admits its public Room separately;
3. a Guest discovers the Room and sends one bounded knock;
4. deterministic hosted state admits, declines and relays without server AI;
5. one Fresh Native Response Session receives only the exact Interaction,
   typed orientation and a sanitized read-only current source snapshot;
6. an untrusted draft returns to the Owner for exact approval;
7. the approved Response is delivered with one public 24-hour/one-interaction
   continuation;
8. a negative test proves that the Private Room remains inaccessible without
   its separate Grant.

The full Private path, notification email, additional continuation presets,
live OpenCode path and broader Agent Guest experience remain optional/future
unless the Owner explicitly restores them to P0.

## Scope rules

- Keep one active R4 product slice and at most one necessary, time-boxed
  Enabler.
- Enabler completion is 0 Product Progress until it unlocks the encounter.
- Preserve the complete causal chain; do not call a partial technical proof
  Presence or Done.
- Cut breadth and flourish before cutting Continuity, Cognition, Agency,
  Presence or a trust boundary.
- P1 starts only after P0 is Green.
- R5 starts after R4 Owner Experience Acceptance, not on a calendar date.
- Progress/Vision sharing reports the gate honestly and grants no additional
  execution authority.

## Required separations

These decisions remain independent even when one person performs both:

- Owner publication and curator admission;
- public Projection and Private Room access;
- technical review and Owner experience acceptance;
- draft generation and Owner-attributed publication;
- local/synthetic proof and production authority.

No roadmap state authorizes production or private server access, public
traffic, real/private Guest data, provider/model calls, credentials, spend,
deployment, publication, messaging, merge or Gate C.

## Progress semantics

- Planned — outcome is defined but not admitted.
- Ready — dependencies and the outcome envelope are approved.
- Building — one outcome is actively being implemented.
- Technical Review — checks and review instructions exist.
- Owner Acceptance — the Owner must experience and judge the result.
- Done — technical and Owner evidence agree.
