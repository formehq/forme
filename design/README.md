## Durable ideas already represented on `main`

- Continuity, Cognition, Bounded Agency, and Controlled Presence form one causal
  Living Project Twin story.
- Codex and OpenCode are replaceable cognition runtimes; Forme owns durable Twin
  state, evidence, corrections, approval, deterministic effects, receipts, and
  projections.
- Runtime output is a proposal, never canonical truth or direct write
  authority.
- Greater agency must preserve authorship, legibility, evidence, correction,
  and rollback.

## Ideas worth retaining for later exploration

- specialized agents as cognitive organs sharing one entity state;
- Incubation and Timing as long-horizon capabilities;
- conflict resolution between cognitive passes before a unified agency
  boundary;
- runtime capability negotiation and golden adapter tests;
- entropy reduction as continuous stewardship rather than periodic cleanup.

These are future design inputs. They do not broaden the August MVP.

## Known superseded assumptions

Do not revive the following without a new owner decision and Control Packet:

- the former 2026-08-15 finish date;
- a fixed `98_Forme/` canonical layout;
- a server-side Projection Agent or server-provided AI dialogue;
- the old `main` migration phases and M0–M5 schedule;
- draft contract files as current production schemas;
- broad mailbox, whole-vault, wake-daemon, or multi-agent implementation scope.

## Promotion rule

Material moves from this archive into `main` only through a small, reviewable
document change that:

1. identifies the current owner-approved claim it supports;
2. labels dated facts and unvalidated hypotheses;
3. removes private source names and machine-local provenance;
4. does not silently change product scope, visibility, trust, or runtime
   authority.
